# Awesome-AI-Governance-Platform

## Top AI Governance Platform Ecosystem



**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**  

*Focused on AI Policy Management, Model Inventory, Risk & Compliance Workflows, Responsible AI Controls & Audit-Ready Governance*  

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **AI Governance**. These systems help organizations inventory AI systems, define and enforce policies, assess risk and fairness, document decisions, and produce evidence for regulators, boards, and internal audit.



**Examples** include Credo AI, Holistic AI, FairNow, Monitaur, Fiddler AI, IBM watsonx.governance, Microsoft AI Governance, DataRobot Governance, SAS AI Governance, Trustible, ModelOp, TruEra, CalypsoAI, and Credal (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Full enterprise AI governance suites are largely commercial. Open building blocks include **model cards**, **AegisAI**-style GRC tools, **ModelScan**, **Evidently**, **guardrails**, fairness toolkits, and NIST AI RMF-aligned templates. This section lists every significant relevant project found.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects



- **[AegisAI](https://github.com/SdSarthak/AegisAI)**  

  Open-source AI-GRC platform oriented to EU AI Act workflows—system registration, risk classification, technical documentation, and related governance artefacts.



- **[AiExponent / RiskForge-style tools](https://github.com/aiexponenthq)**  

  Open utilities for AI risk screening, risk management files, and compliance documentation mapped to regulatory obligations.



- **[Model cards & documentation toolkits](https://github.com/huggingface/model-card-toolkit)**  

  Open standards and tools for documenting intended use, metrics, limitations, and risks—foundational governance evidence.



- **[ModelScan (Protect AI)](https://github.com/protectai/modelscan)**  

  Open-source scanner for unsafe model serialization—supply-chain control for governed model intake.



- **[Evidently](https://github.com/evidentlyai/evidently)**  

  Open ML/LLM monitoring for drift, performance, and quality—operational signals for governed production systems.



- **[NeMo Guardrails, LLM Guard](https://github.com/NVIDIA/NeMo-Guardrails)**  

  Open runtime policy and safety controls whose configuration and logs support governance evidence packages.



- **[Fairlearn, AIF360 & fairness toolkits](https://github.com/fairlearn/fairlearn)**  

  Open libraries for measuring and mitigating bias as part of responsible AI and model risk processes.



- **[NIST AI RMF playbooks & open checklists](https://github.com/search?q=NIST+AI+RMF+OR+AI+governance+open+source)**  

  Community templates and implementations aligned to NIST AI Risk Management Framework and similar voluntary standards.



### Additional Strong Open-Source Options



- **Documentation**: Model cards and system cards as minimum governance artefacts.

- **Inventory**: Lightweight open registries for AI systems, owners, and risk tiers.

- **Technical controls**: ModelScan + guardrails + Evidently as demonstrable mitigations.

- **Fairness**: Fairlearn/AIF360 in validation pipelines.

- **Composable stacks**: Inventory → risk classification → model card → monitoring → policy rails → audit log.

- Commercial platforms still lead in multi-framework policy engines, workflow, and enterprise reporting.



**Frameworks for building custom systems**:  

Open **model cards**, **ModelScan**, **Evidently**, **fairness toolkits**, and **guardrails** form a practical governance toolkit.  

**AegisAI**-class projects aim at fuller AI-GRC workflows.  

Commercial platforms (Credo AI, Holistic AI, IBM watsonx.governance, ModelOp, Monitaur, etc.) deliver process, scale, and audit support.  

Many organizations combine commercial governance platforms with open technical controls. Fully open governance programs are feasible for smaller estates; regulated enterprises typically need commercial depth.

## Disclaimer



- This is a **community-curated** list — not exhaustive and not an endorsement.

- AI governance is jurisdiction- and sector-specific (EU AI Act, sector rules, internal policy). Software does not replace legal advice, independent validation, or formal conformity procedures. High-risk systems may require notified bodies or external audit.

- Open-source tools help structure documentation and controls but do not guarantee regulatory acceptance. Commercial platforms shift process and support burden to the vendor. Always involve legal, risk, and compliance stakeholders.
